### Hey, I'm Soham

Math & Computing at NSUT, Delhi. I like projects where the hard part is the data or the algorithm.

**Things I've built**
- **[Shade Routes](https://github.com/sohammawai/shade-routes)**: finds the shadiest walking route across Delhi NCR. Shade for 776K street segments at every hour of the year, ray-cast with Numba and served from AWS Lambda. [Try it](https://main.dczwtg7so4eis.amplifyapp.com/app.html)
- **[Tabula](https://github.com/sohammawai/tabula)**: an AlphaZero-style chess engine (neural net + MCTS), about 2590 Elo against Stockfish's limited mode. *In progress.*
- **Diabetes Risk Triage**: sorts 229K survey records into risk tiers using rough sets, fuzzy logic and neural nets. *In progress.*
- **[DFA Minimizer](https://github.com/sohammawai/dfa-minimizer)**: an animated, in-browser walkthrough of the Myhill–Nerode table-filling algorithm.
